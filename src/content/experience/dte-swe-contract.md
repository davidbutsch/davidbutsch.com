At Dream Team Elite I developed and deployed a company website consisting of a payment processor, player gallery, and subscription management portal.

This was my first experience working with production software and getting familiar with real-world user feedback. I also played a large role in technical support. When customers had an issue logging in or switching payment plans, I was there to implement solutions or provide assistance.

The website waws built from the ground up (like pre-claude ground up), using React and Material-UI. Theming was a back-and-forth feedback loop. We would try new things and land on a final product. I also designed the logo with Illustrator and took brand photos as seen on the landing page.

![](https://assets.davidbutsch.com/uploads/1762317284459.jpeg)
<p align="center" style="font-style: italic;">Dream Team Elite Landing Page</p>

Additionally, this was my first chance working with AWS. The backend API was containerized and launched in AWS Fargate. I used AWS Cognito for user authentication, and S3 for asset/uploads storage. I also implementing an administrator dashboard for image and media content uploads. The dashboard also contained a customer billing overview pulled from the Stripe API.

Stripe was used for customer payments, using their React library with custom components to fit the company brand.
